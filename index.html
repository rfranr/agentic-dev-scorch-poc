Scorch

Preparing the battlefield…

This game requires Canvas 2D support.
Aim the active tank

Aim toward the other tank, then fire.

Angle is measured above the direction toward your opponent: 45° arcs forward, 90° fires straight up. Adjust sliders with the mouse or arrow keys, then press Fire. Aim close to a tank to destroy it. Shots leave craters; tanks settle onto the remaining ground. Each player keeps their own aim.

One shot at a time. No wind. New match restarts at any time.
