Aim the active tank

Aim toward the other tank, then fire.

Aim along the dotted guide. 45° fires toward your opponent; 90° fires up. More power sends the shot farther. The guide shows launch direction, not the full flight path.

How to play

Cyan is Player 1; coral is Player 2. Labels show player and health; the ground ring marks the active tank. Adjust sliders with the mouse or arrow keys, then press Fire. Angles above 90° fire backward. Nearby blasts are lethal. Unsupported earth falls as rubble; tanks fall and lose health on hard landings. Wait for all movement to settle before the next turn. Each player keeps their aim. No wind. New match restarts at any time.
